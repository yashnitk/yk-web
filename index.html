01 / RESEARCH

Energy research, connected.

Dr. Kashyap’s work lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, renewable energy systems, smart grids, and airborne wind energy. His research integrates machine learning, remote sensing, numerical modelling, real-time simulation, and prototype-based validation for intelligent and sustainable energy systems.

☀️

AI-based Solar Forecasting

Machine learning, cloud motion vectors and remote sensing for solar energy nowcasting and PV planning.

🪁

Airborne Wind Energy

Kite-based wind power systems, real-time emulation and flight-control oriented energy conversion research.

🔋

EV & Storage Systems

Hybrid energy management, battery systems, BMS concepts and intelligent power allocation for EVs.
